# Navigator Genome Pilot — Demo Video Narration v2 (Daniel voice)

Target ~2:45, hard cap 3:00. Research-use framing + disclaimer in section 1; honest about limits in outro.

1. INTRO+DISCLAIMER
This is Navigator — an eight-agent research tool for interpreting rare-disease genomes. Before anything else: Navigator is a research tool. It is not a diagnostic system, and it does not give medical advice. It assembles evidence for scientists and clinicians to review.

2. PROBLEM+TEAM
Diagnosing a rare disease can take years. A single genome holds millions of variants, and the true answer is usually buried among them. Navigator puts eight specialist A-I agents on the problem together — intake, variant filtering, phenotype mapping, literature, databases, case matching, an independent red team, and a dossier writer — all coordinated by a Director.

3. THE LAB
Here is the team at work, in a live research lab. Each scientist runs their own part of the investigation against real biomedical sources — Europe P-M-C, MyVariant, ClinVar, genome aggregation data, and the Human Phenotype Ontology. Hover over any researcher, and they will tell you what they found and how confident they are.

4. RUN+EVIDENCE
On a real case, the agents light up as they work. Every claim they make carries a knowledge label — verified, implemented, or only a hypothesis — along with a confidence level. Missing database records are treated as absent, not benign. And the red team independently tries to knock down the leading candidate before it is ever reported.

5. DOSSIER
The result is an evidence dossier: the ranked candidate variant, the supporting and the contradicting evidence, the uncertainty spelled out in plain language, and a downloadable P-D-F that a researcher can actually use.

6. BENCHMARK
Does it work? We tested Navigator on fifteen already-solved, published rare-disease cases — from cystic fibrosis and sickle cell, to Huntington's, Marfan, and Wilson disease — with the answers hidden. Running on Meta's Muse Spark model, Navigator ranked the correct gene and variant first in every single case. Fifteen out of fifteen.

7. PRODUCTION+OUTRO
Navigator runs on Meta models today, with A-W-S as its infrastructure backbone. It is live, it is public, and it is open source. This is a research instrument for the people who hunt down rare diseases — built to assemble the evidence, and honest about what it does not yet know.
